# Image Prompt Workflow

After the article drafts are approved, do not generate the images yourself.

Instead, provide 1 strong AI image prompt per article.

Rules:

- keep the visuals practical, modern, and business-friendly
- align them to Kampala, Nairobi, and wider African business contexts
- human models are optional
- if human models are included, they should look African, dressed in smart casual clothing, with subtle hints of Ankara fabric
- avoid stereotype-heavy or costume-like visuals
- where a non-human concept image works better, prefer that instead

For each prompt, include:

- image title
- full generation prompt
- one-line note describing the intended feeling or message
