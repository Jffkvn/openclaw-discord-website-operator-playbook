# Article Draft Prompt

I have chosen these article ideas.

Briefly say whether you agree or disagree with the selection, then immediately turn them into website-ready article drafts.

Rules:

- keep the tone conversational, practical, and non-technical
- write for business owners and managers, not AI specialists
- if a "why this may not be practical yet" angle genuinely improves an article, include it
- do not force that angle into every article
- do not edit website files yet
